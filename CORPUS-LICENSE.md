# Corpus attribution and license

Mexilex source code is MIT licensed, but **the lexical data is not**.

The initial corpus is derived from Spanish-language lexical entries in the **English edition of Wiktionary**, extracted by Kaikki/Wiktextract. Entries are authored by Wiktionary contributors and remain under the applicable upstream licenses, including **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA)** and **GNU Free Documentation License (GFDL)**. Do not treat the corpus as MIT-licensed, and do not remove attribution or share-alike obligations when reusing it.

- Original contributors: https://en.wiktionary.org/
- Wiktionary copyright and license terms: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
- Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- GNU FDL: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html
- Structured extraction: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/
- Wiktextract: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

The normalized corpus includes transformations to source structure, labels and record identifiers. Reusers should verify the exact licensing of source material they redistribute and preserve modification/attribution notices. The `corpus/upstream.sha256` file (generated during the initial seed workflow) records the integrity of the downloaded upstream file; the output manifest records the extraction family and counts.
